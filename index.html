Purpose

A formal act of recognition before birth.

01

Recognition

The passport records the existence of an unborn child as a life already present within a family, a history, and a future.

02

Dignity

The document expresses the principle that unborn lives deserve care, respect, and consideration in the public imagination.

03

Continuity

From conception to birth and beyond, every life belongs to a continuous human story worthy of being acknowledged.

Declaration

International Conception Passport Charter

The International Conception Passport affirms that a conceived child may be symbolically counted, named, and welcomed before birth.

  • To provide families with a solemn record of conception.
  • To promote respect for unborn human life.
  • To encourage public awareness of the responsibilities owed to future generations.
  • To create a dignified ceremonial document that can be preserved by the family.

“Every life begins before it is seen by the world. Recognition is not merely administrative; it is moral, familial, and human.”
